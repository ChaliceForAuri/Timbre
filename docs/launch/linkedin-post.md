## Copy

I built a dictation app for the Mac that never touches the internet. It's called Timbre, it's free, and today it's public.

Hold a key and talk. Let go, and cleaned-up text lands wherever your cursor is: filler gone, punctuation right, your phrasing intact. Not a transcript of what you said — what you meant to type.

Three keys, no window, no tour:
→ Hold right Option: dictate into any app.
→ Tap left Option: hear a selection read back.
→ Hold right Command on a selection and say fix, shorten, explain or plain. Say a to-do with nothing selected and it lands in Apple's Reminders.

The part I care about most: out of the box, Timbre makes zero network requests. Not "we don't sell your data". None. Speech recognition and the cleanup both run on Apple's on-device models. Point Little Snitch at it and watch nothing happen. Turn on update checks and it makes exactly one request, to my own site, carrying nothing about you. Every request it can ever make is listed on the site.

No account. No subscription. Notarized by Apple. Source on GitHub.

[I'm a Flutter and React Native developer who had never written Swift. Timbre is the project I learned it on, and every decision along the way — what to build, what to refuse — is written down as a decision record you can read.]

It needs macOS 26 on Apple Silicon with Apple Intelligence on.

Download: https://timbre.hugopretorius.dev
