# The 30-second demo

- **Kind:** script
- **Status:** to record
- **Order:** 3

One take, screen only, no voice-over — the captions carry it. Record at 1920×1200 with the menu bar hidden, light mode, a plain desktop. Every shot is an action the viewer could repeat in the next minute.

## Shot list

| At | Shot | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Mail compose window, cursor in the body. Hold right ⌥; the pill appears: "Waking the mic…" then live words with filler. | Hold a key. Talk. |
| 0:06 | Release. "Cleaning up…", then the clean sentence pastes in. | Let go. Cleaned-up text lands where your cursor is. |
| 0:11 | Select "timber kit" in the sentence, hold right ⌘, say "fix". It becomes "TimbreKit". | Hold on a selection. Say fix. |
| 0:16 | Select "ADR", hold right ⌘, say "explain". The card slides in: "Architecture Decision Record — from your dictionary". | Or explain. Your definitions answer first. |
| 0:21 | Nothing selected. Hold right ⌘, say "call the dentist Thursday". Reminders opens on the Timbre list with the item dated. | Say a to-do. It's in Reminders, on your phone too. |
| 0:26 | Select the paragraph, tap left ⌥. The reading pill appears; the cursor moves through the text. | Tap to hear it back. |
| 0:29 | Cut to the wordmark over the keyboard illustration, rainbow stripe. | Zero network requests. Free. timbre.hugopretorius.dev |

## Before recording

- Settings › Dictionary: "timber kit → TimbreKit" taught; "ADR" defined.
- Reminders open in a second Space so the switch is one swipe.
- Enhanced voice installed (Settings › Reading says which).
- Rehearse the right ⌘ holds: the mode arms after 350 ms, and "fix" fires on the word.
- `nettop -p Timbre` running in a Terminal window off-screen — useful for a still, not for the clip.

## After
